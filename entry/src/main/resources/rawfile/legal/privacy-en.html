MiYouYou Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 20, 2025

Cain Luo has released "MiYouYou" as a free application, provided by Cain Luo (the "Developer") via the Huawei AppGallery. This page informs you of the policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of data by this application.

Collection and Use of Information

All user data (including MiHoYo account Cookies) is stored exclusively on the User's local HarmonyOS device and will not be uploaded to the Developer's servers or any third-party servers.

This application uses the account credentials provided by the User to request game data from MiHoYo Co., Ltd. (Shanghai)'s public API. The retrieved data is displayed locally only and is not used for any other purpose.

This application is an unofficial tool and has no affiliation with MiHoYo Co., Ltd. The Developer does not actively collect any personally identifiable information.

Log Data

This application generates runtime logs on the local device to assist with troubleshooting. These log files are stored only on the User's device and are not automatically uploaded to any server. Users may manually export log files from within the application to report issues to the Developer.

Cookies

Certain features of this application rely on MiHoYo account Cookies. These Cookies are entered manually by the User or obtained through the in-app login process, and are stored only on the User's local HarmonyOS device. The Developer does not collect any Cookies data from users.

Service Providers

This application is distributed via the Huawei AppGallery. The Developer may cooperate with Huawei Technologies Co., Ltd. as required by the Huawei Developer Service Agreement for the following purposes:

  • To provide this application on the Huawei AppGallery;
  • Other matters as required by Huawei Technologies Co., Ltd.

The Developer declares that the Developer does not have access to collect user personal information.

Links to Other Sites

This application may contain the following links:

  • The GitHub repository link of this application;
  • URLs related to MiHoYo Co., Ltd. (Shanghai) and its related services.

Children's Privacy

The Developer does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. The Developer encourages all children to never submit any personally identifiable information through this application. The Developer encourages parents and legal guardians to monitor their children's internet usage and to help enforce this policy. If you have reason to believe that a child has provided personally identifiable information to the Developer through this application, please contact the Developer. You must also be at least 16 years of age to consent to the processing of your personally identifiable information in your country or region.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

The Developer may update this privacy policy from time to time. It is recommended that you review this page periodically for any changes. The Developer will notify you of any changes by posting the new privacy policy on this page.
